# Uninstall.io

> Uninstall.io was a mobile "uninstall intelligence" platform (Songline Analytics, Bengaluru/Singapore, founded 2015) whose iOS and Android SDKs tracked app uninstalls, surfaced uninstall reasons, and drove re-install campaigns. Backed by 500 Startups India, Rajan Anandan, and Hive Data; customers included Snapdeal, Axis Bank, Zalora, Zivame, and BankBazaar. Acquired by Singapore healthtech company Mobile Health in July 2018. The uninstall.io domain is now parked for sale and the company no longer operates a public API surface.
